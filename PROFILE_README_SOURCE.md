# Hawkar — Research & Engineering

I build auditable experimental systems at the intersection of algorithmic research, reproducible verification, embedded computing, and local-first AI.

My current public work is organized around a small number of projects with explicit claim boundaries, replayable evidence, and preserved negative results.

## Flagship research

### [Janus-Fundamentum](https://github.com/Hawkar-usls/Janus-Fundamentum)

Proof-search laboratory for computational complexity, finite-field subspace arrangements, and proof-carrying verification.

Current publication track: a scoped endpoint-compression / ternary dynamic-programming result for repeated geometric subspace classes. The theorem is frozen for external mathematical review; historical world priority and external independent replication are not claimed.

### [AIFC](https://github.com/Hawkar-usls/AIFC)

**Auditable Independent-Future Challenge** — a fail-closed protocol for testing claims that information was fixed before an independently generated future random target.

The project focuses on causal ordering, immutable preregistration, entropy evidence, multiplicity accounting, anytime-valid statistics, witness/quorum semantics, provenance, and independent replay. Physical retrocausality is not claimed or observed.

### [janus-io-public](https://github.com/Hawkar-usls/janus-io-public)

Experimental systems and measurement work on reducing unnecessary Proof-of-Work computation, thermal stress, and unaccountable work while preserving standard verification semantics.

The strongest public result is a replicated native admission-wave segmentation experiment. Energy savings, profitability, SHA-256 predictability, and extended hardware lifetime are not claimed without separate evidence.

### [janus-distributed-ai-swarm](https://github.com/Hawkar-usls/janus-distributed-ai-swarm)

Embedded/distributed firmware for a heterogeneous ESP32 and M5Stack swarm with explicit protocol boundaries, telemetry, recovery paths, observer semantics, and CI-built sketches.

## Creative technology

### [Janus_Genesis](https://github.com/Hawkar-usls/Janus_Genesis)

A local-first persistent interactive world and AI-native game experiment focused on player agency, free-form actions, portable saves, local model support, and non-authoritative AI assistance.

This is a creative-technology project, separate from the research evidence lines above.

## Research principles

- Freeze claims before promoting them.
- Preserve negative results and counterexamples.
- Separate machine verification from scientific validation.
- Treat CI as evidence about software execution, not as external peer review.
- Prefer independently reproducible artifacts over narrative claims.
- Keep speculative or metaphorical modules separate from established results.
